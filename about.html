Our Story

From a Bold Idea to a Trusted Name

Biochem Technologies was incorporated in August 2023 by a team of textile chemical professionals who had spent years working with mills across India. The founding insight was straightforward: most small and mid-sized textile processors were either overpaying for imported chemicals or settling for inconsistent local alternatives.

We set out to change that. Starting with a focused range of pretreatment auxiliaries, we rapidly expanded into dyeing, printing, and finishing — building each formulation from the ground up based on real feedback from production floors, not laboratory ideals.

Today, from our registered offices in Kolkata and Ghaziabad, we serve 50+ mills and processing houses. Every product in our portfolio carries a field-tested guarantee: it was trialled on live machines before it reached your warehouse.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every decision we make — from formulation to delivery — is guided by four principles.

Quality First

Every batch is tested against specification before it leaves our warehouse. We never compromise consistency for cost — because your production schedule depends on it.

Responsive Service

No long ticketing queues. When a mill faces a production issue, our technical team is reachable directly — same day, every time.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden charges, no bait-and-switch. We quote what we mean and invoice what we quote — helping mills plan their costs with certainty.

Sustainable Chemistry

APEO-free and low-VOC formulations are not an afterthought — they are built into our product roadmap. We believe green processing and high performance are not mutually exclusive.
