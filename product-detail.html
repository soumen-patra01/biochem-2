Biowet Ultra

A low-foam wetting and scouring agent engineered for rapid, uniform absorbency on cellulosics and blends — in both continuous ranges and exhaust machines.

Product Overview

Uniform absorbency, right first time

Biowet Ultra combines rapid wetting power with strong detergency, removing natural waxes, oils and residual size in a single bath. Its low-foam profile makes it safe for jet, soft-flow and continuous applications at high running speeds.

Uniform absorbency after pretreatment is the single biggest factor in right-first-time dyeing. Mills using Biowet Ultra report fewer shade corrections, better peroxide bath stability and measurable reductions in re-process rates on both knits and wovens.

Applications

Recommended process flow

Suitable for cotton, viscose and blends on jet, soft-flow, jigger, winch and continuous ranges — for desizing assistance, scouring and combined scour-bleach routes.

STEP 01

Dose & Fill

Add 0.5–2.0 g/L Biowet Ultra with alkali to the bath at 40 °C.

STEP 02

Raise Temperature

Heat to 95–98 °C (exhaust) or steam pass (continuous).

STEP 03

Hold

Run 30–45 min for full wax and size removal.

STEP 04

Rinse & Verify

Hot and cold rinse; verify absorbency by drop test.
