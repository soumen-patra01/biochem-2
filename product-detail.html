ZETAWET-SWLF

Zetawet SWLF Conc

High-concentration sulfosuccinate-free low-foam wetting & re-wetting agent for exhaust, semi-continuous, and continuous processing of cellulosic and synthetic fibres.

Product Overview

Superior wetting for defect-free downstream processing

Zetawet SWLF Conc is a breakthrough in textile treatment, offering a revolutionary solution for fabric pre-treatment. This highly efficient re-wetting agent and detergent boasts exceptional qualities, making it an ideal choice for exhaust, semi-continuous, and continuous processing of cellulosic and synthetic fibres.

It enhances fabric hydrophilicity, ensuring optimal absorption for subsequent dyeing and printing processes. Its shear-stable nature guarantees seamless performance without the risk of separation. This APEO-free formulation prioritises environmental sustainability while delivering outstanding bio-elimination properties. Its pumpable liquid form ensures effortless handling, while its compatibility with alpha-amylase enzymes, oxidative bleaching agents, alkalis, and acids underscores its versatility.
